# Fact-check: Sweden baby names Short

Method: web search only. Page fetches were blocked (en.wikipedia.org and thelocal.com did not resolve in this session), so verdicts rest on search-result summaries. Claim 2 details and the Metallica final outcome are only lightly confirmed.

## 1. Skatteverket approval / naming law - TRUE
- Lag (2016:1013) om personnamn replaced Namnlag (1982:670) on 1 July 2017.
- First names are approved by Skatteverket. A name is refused if it could cause offence, could reasonably burden the bearer, or is otherwise unsuitable as a first name.
- Sources:
  - https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-lagar/dokument/_sfs-2016-1013
  - https://skatteverket.se/privat/folkbokforing/namn/nypersonnamnlagfrom1juli2017.4.5c281c7015abecc2e202edad.html
  - https://lagen.nu/dom/hfd/2019:57
- Lines 4-5 are OK. Optional nuance: the rule is "could burden the bearer", so "hurt the kid" is a fair paraphrase.

## 2. 43-character name / "A" - TRUE with caveats (lines 6-12)
- The boy was born in 1991. His parents were fined 5,000 kr for not registering a name by his fifth birthday. They submitted the 43-character string as a protest, said to be pronounced "Albin", and offered "A" (also "Albin"). The tax agency rejected both.
- Caveats:
  - I could not confirm the exact year the fine was issued from a fetched page. Secondary sources date the case to 1996.
  - Sources differ on whether the fine was imposed by a court or by the agency. Line 6 ("fined... in 1996") is safe.
  - "The fine stayed" (line 9) is not directly confirmed. Reword to avoid asserting an appeal result.
  - The sources say the "A" offer was also rejected. Whether it came after the long name is not exactly documented, so "next try" is the loosest wording.
- Sources:
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naming_law_in_Sweden
  - https://www.boingboing.net/2008/02/20/swedish-couple-fined.html
  - https://nowiknow.com/?p=6838
- Suggested rewords (same length, no names):
  - Line 9: "Rejected. And they still got fined." (replaces "Rejected. And the fine stayed.")
  - Line 11: "They also offered just "A". Also pronounced... Albin." (replaces "Their next try? Just the letter "A". Also pronounced... Albin.")

## 3. Metallica - TRUE (line 13)
- A couple in Kungalv, around March-April 2007, wanted "Metallica" for their daughter. The tax agency objected, calling it "gross and unflattering". A Gothenburg county administrative court sided with the family. The agency was reported to have appealed and later dropped its objection. The final step is lightly sourced.
- Sources:
  - https://www.thelocal.com/20070403/6895/
  - https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/1293923
  - https://www.theregister.com/2007/04/04/metallica/
- Line 13 is acceptable as is. A safer same-length alternative: "Meanwhile, another Swedish family fought for the name Metallica... and got it."
