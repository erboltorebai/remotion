In Switzerland, it's illegal to own just one guinea pig.
Where are we going today?
Switzerland! Mountains, chocolate... and very serious pet laws.
Guinea pigs are herd animals. Alone, they get lonely, stressed, and sad.
So in 2008, the Swiss government stepped in.
Social pets must have company of their own kind.
One guinea pig? Wrong. You need at least two.
And it gets weirder.
What happens when one of them dies?
The survivor can't be left alone... so Swiss people can rent a guinea pig.
A temporary best friend, until the very end.
And when the last one goes, the buddy goes back home to its herd.
